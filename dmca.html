DMCA Policy

Totally Science respects intellectual property rights. If you believe material available through this website infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to enforce, you may submit a copyright complaint for review.

Information to Include in a Copyright Notice

To help us evaluate a request, provide enough information to identify both the copyrighted work and the material you want reviewed. A notice should include:

  • Your full name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL or sufficiently precise location of the material at issue.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or applicable law.
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

How to Submit a Notice

Submit your request through the Contact Us page and use a clear subject such as DMCA Takedown Request. Include the exact page URL whenever possible so the reported material can be located quickly.

Review and Removal

After receiving a sufficiently detailed request, we may review the reported material and take appropriate action, which can include removing or disabling access to content when warranted.

Counter-Notification

If material you submitted or control was removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal resulted from a mistake or misidentification, you may contact us with the affected URL, an explanation of the issue, and the information needed to evaluate your request.

Misrepresentations

Copyright notices and counter-notices should be submitted in good faith. Knowingly making material misrepresentations in a copyright complaint may have legal consequences.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as the site, contact process, or applicable requirements change.
